## Findings for the coordinator grade of group G

- **G05-1 (FAIL, font scale 2.0):** the ALL SESSIONS / FAVORITE SESSIONS tab labels break mid-word and their second line is clipped ("ALL SES / SIONS", "FAVORIT / E SESSIO"), so required labels are clipped. At 1.3 the labels wrap cleanly ("ALL / SESSIONS").
- **G05-2 (FAIL, font scale 2.0):** the "Favorite" row button text is wider than its button outline and spills past both edges. Detail and filter-modal frames at 2.0 are usable (text wraps, page scrolls, all buttons visible).
- **G06 (no defect):** density 504 keeps search, rows, filter modal, dialogs and detail usable with no off-screen controls.
- **G07-1 (FAIL, dark mode, cold and live switch):** the app keeps its light pages (readable content, dialogs and selected states), but the status-bar clock and icons are invisible: the system draws light icons for the dark theme over the app's white area. The status bar becomes visible only under a dialog scrim. RN07 recorded the same class of defect as F-4.
- **G08 (BLOCKED for keyboard/insets):** this AVD's Gboard shows only its floating toolbar (mic, delete, search, emoji, menu) when the SearchBar is focused, even with `show_ime_with_hard_keyboard=1`; it overlays the list without insets (RN07 D12 precedent). Header, search and content are not covered by the status or navigation bars; scroll edges are reachable.
- **G01/G02 (BLOCKED context):** the full keyboard never appeared, so the keyboard-dismissal Back and keyboard-visible interactions could not be exercised.
- **Cold-start frames:** five `*-cold-top` frames caught the NativeScript launch screen before the first page rendered; this is capture timing, not a defect.
- **G09:** background/resume of schedule and detail, and the separate force-stop cold reset, show no duplicate screen, spurious filter change or duplicate favorite.
